Questions.

The short answers.

01

What is a Cloud AI Computer?

A full computer (operating system, apps, and assistant) that runs on your own hardware and opens in your browser, anywhere. Behind a secure tunnel, it becomes your own cloud. Sign in and your computer is right there.
02

Is my data really mine?

Yes. Everything Liv learns about you, every file, every chat, is encrypted and stored in your computer's space. Open source means anyone can verify there's no telemetry. And it all lives on your own hardware from day one.
03

Which AI does Liv use?

Bring your own. Liv works with Claude, Gemini, and local models. You choose. We never charge you for tokens, and the keys are yours.
04

Can I use Livinity on my phone?

Open it in any browser. Liv remembers your conversation between every screen: phone, laptop, tablet, IDE.
05

Do I have to be technical?

No. One command on any Linux box and the installer does the rest. If you can use a website, you can use Livinity. Builders get an open API on top.
06

What if I want to leave?

Export everything and take it anywhere. It was on your own machine all along. Livinity is open source, so the door is always unlocked.
