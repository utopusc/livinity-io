01 · Getting started

From install to signed in, in three steps.

Step 1

Install

Run the installer for your platform. macOS opens a DMG, Windows runs a signed setup, Linux installs via .deb.

Step 2

Sign in

Use your livinity.io account. The agent registers as a device — manage and revoke from your dashboard.

Step 3

Connect

Your Cloud AI Computer appears in the menu bar. Open files, chat with Liv, launch apps — wherever you are.
