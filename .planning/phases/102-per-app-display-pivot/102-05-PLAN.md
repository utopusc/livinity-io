<objective>
Bring native apps (Phase 101-03 — VSCode, Antigravity, etc.) into Phase 102 parity. Replace WM_CLASS polling on shared `:1` with per-app Xvfb display allocation: each native app gets its own `:N` from DisplayAllocator, its own Xvfb (optionally with fluxbox since GUI apps often need a WM, unlike Chrome --app=URL), and its own x11vnc display-target stream.

No profile seeding (native binaries manage their own state).

Output: Modified `apps/native-app-binder.ts` + extended tests + updated `apps/native-routes.ts` spawn path.
</objective>
