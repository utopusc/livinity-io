  <name>Task 2: systemd unit template</name>
  <action>
    Create `livos/scripts/systemd/liv-claw-gateway.service`:
    ```ini
    [Unit]
    Description=Liv AI Gateway (openclaw gateway, port 18789)
    After=network-online.target livos.service
    Wants=network-online.target

    [Service]
    Type=simple
    User=bruce
    Group=bruce
    WorkingDirectory=/opt/livos/packages/liv-claw-gateway
    Environment=PORT=18789
    Environment=NODE_ENV=production
    EnvironmentFile=-/opt/livos/.env
    ExecStart=/usr/bin/env bash /opt/livos/packages/liv-claw-gateway/start.sh
    Restart=on-failure
    RestartSec=5
    StandardOutput=journal
    StandardError=journal

    [Install]
    WantedBy=multi-user.target
    ```
