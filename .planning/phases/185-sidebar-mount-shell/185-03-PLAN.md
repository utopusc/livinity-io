<objective>
Add two UX enhancements to the split layout from Plans 185-01/02:

1. **Mobile collapse**: When `useIsMobile()` returns true AND `sidebarOpen` is false
   (default on mobile), the left pane is hidden. A hamburger icon button in the top-left
   of the right pane header toggles visibility. Desktop: `sidebarOpen` defaults to `true`
   so the sidebar is always visible.

2. **AddItemModal trigger**: A small `+` icon button at the top of the left pane
   (above the SidebarTree, inside the left-pane div) toggles `addModalOpen` state.
   `<AddItemModal>` is mounted at route level; its `open` prop is wired to `addModalOpen`.

Purpose: Mobile users can access the full AI Chat route without the sidebar eating
screen space. The `+` Add button makes item creation discoverable directly from
the sidebar pane (per CONTEXT.md Plan 185-03 decision).

Output: Updated `index.tsx` + 4 new green assertions (total 32 in ai-chat.test.tsx).
</objective>
